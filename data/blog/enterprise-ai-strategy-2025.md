The gap between AI proof-of-concept and production deployment remains one of the biggest challenges for enterprises. According to industry research, only 10-15% of AI projects make it to production. This article explores proven strategies to bridge that gap.

## The Production Readiness Framework

Moving from PoC to production requires a structured approach across four dimensions:

### 1. Data Infrastructure
Your AI models are only as good as your data pipeline. Ensure you have robust data ingestion, cleaning, validation, and versioning before scaling any model.

### 2. Model Operations (MLOps)
Implement CI/CD for ML: automated testing, model registry, A/B testing frameworks, and monitoring dashboards. Tools like MLflow, Kubeflow, and Weights & Biases are essential.

### 3. Governance & Compliance
Establish model governance early: bias detection, explainability (SHAP, LIME), audit trails, and regulatory compliance checks specific to your industry.

### 4. Organizational Alignment
Success requires buy-in from stakeholders. Create an AI Center of Excellence, define clear KPIs, and establish feedback loops between data teams and business units.

## Key Takeaways

- Start with high-impact, low-risk use cases
- Invest in data infrastructure before model complexity
- Automate everything: testing, deployment, monitoring
- Measure business outcomes, not just model accuracy
